# Market & User Research for Sports Betting Apps

## Executive Summary

The sports betting market is experiencing explosive growth, with the US market reaching $149.6 billion in total wagered amount in 2024 (23.5% increase from 2023). DraftKings and FanDuel dominate with approximately 67-80% combined market share, but emerging competitors like bet365, Fanatics, and Hard Rock are gaining traction.

## Market Size & Growth

### Current Market Statistics
- **US Total Handle (2024)**: $149.6 billion (+23.5% from 2023)
- **Gross Gaming Revenue (2024)**: $13.7 billion (+24.4% from 2023)
- **Tax Revenue (2024)**: $2.8 billion (+32% from 2023)
- **Legal Jurisdictions**: 39 states + Washington D.C. + Puerto Rico
- **North America Online Gambling Market**: $11.86 billion (2022) → projected $34.69 billion (2032) at 11.3% CAGR
- **Fantasy Sports Market**: $28.95 billion (2024) → projected $67.03 billion (2033)

## Competitor Analysis

### Tier 1 Dominators
**DraftKings** (Market Leader)
- Market share: ~35-40% of combined duopoly
- Strengths: Industry-leading live betting interface, comprehensive stats, excellent rewards program, broader range of sports
- Weaknesses: Basic app design, cluttered interface, slower withdrawals
- Available in 25 states
- DFS rating: 5.0/5

**FanDuel** (Co-Leader)
- Market share: ~35-40% of combined duopoly
- Strengths: User-friendly app (4.8/5 App Store rating), faster withdrawals, strong promotions, live streaming platform (FanDuel TV+)
- Weaknesses: Weaker customer support, fewer niche sports, higher minimum deposit ($10 vs DK's $5)
- Available in 23 states
- Better for same-game parlay customization

### Tier 2 Challengers (Gaining Market Share)
**bet365**
- Gaining significant market share (6% combined with Fanatics)
- Strengths: Best pricing (5.3% overround), established global brand
- Considering potential sale or IPO

**Fanatics**
- Rapid growth, highest proportion of female bettors
- Strengths: Excellent uptime (87.1%), aggressive marketing
- Part of broader sports merchandise ecosystem

**Hard Rock Digital**
- Strengths: Best uptime during March Madness (90.1%), highest green time (86.2%)
- Strong operational performance metrics

### Tier 3 Traditional Players
**BetMGM** (MGM Resorts)
- Market share declining against challengers
- Good uptime performance (90.1% during March Madness)

**Caesars Sportsbook** (Caesars Entertainment)
- Market share declining
- Operational challenges (74.6% uptime during March Madness)

**ESPN BET** (PENN Entertainment)
- Largest proportion of younger users (21-34)
- Struggling with uptime (74% during March Madness)

## User Demographics & Behavior

### Core Demographics
**Age Distribution:**
- 21-34 years: Highest frequency and bet size
- 35-64 years: Moderate participation
- 65+ years: Highest concentration on Caesars and BetMGM

**Gender Split:**
- Sports Bettors: 60% male, 39% female
- Fantasy Players: 64% male, 35% female
- Fanatics has highest female participation

**Income & Education:**
- Sports Bettors: 63% make over $50,000, 79% college degree or higher
- Fantasy Players: 65% make over $50,000, 84% college degree or higher

**Participation Rates:**
- 19-23% of Americans have bet on sports in past year
- 37% of general population have placed any bet
- 49% of sports fans have placed bets
- 30% of 18-29 year olds in Massachusetts have bet (most occasionally, small amounts)

### Betting Behavior Patterns
**Popular Betting Methods:**
1. With friends/family (15%)
2. In-person at casinos (8%)
3. Online betting apps (6%)

**Frequency:**
- 48% of lifetime bettors placed bet in last 6 months
- 55% of sports fans bet in last 6 months
- 71% of avid fans bet in last 6 months

**Popular Sports:**
- NFL betting most popular
- Fantasy football: 79% of fantasy players participate

**Bet Types:**
- Parlays most popular with younger bettors
- Same-game parlays showing strong growth

## Key Success Factors

### User Experience Priorities
1. **Mobile-First Design**: 4.8+ App Store ratings essential
2. **Fast Withdrawals**: Critical competitive advantage
3. **Live Streaming**: Integrated content drives engagement
4. **Real-Time Updates**: Instant score and odds updates
5. **Simple Onboarding**: Streamlined registration and verification

### Operational Excellence
1. **Uptime Performance**: 85%+ uptime required for competitiveness
2. **Competitive Pricing**: Low overround (5.3-5.8%) essential
3. **Diverse Sports Coverage**: 18+ sports markets standard
4. **Payment Flexibility**: Multiple payment methods (PayPal, Venmo, cards)
5. **Customer Support**: Live chat, email, comprehensive help centers

### Growth Strategies
1. **Aggressive Marketing**: High customer acquisition spending
2. **Welcome Bonuses**: "Bet $5, Get $200+" promotions standard
3. **Loyalty Programs**: Rewards systems drive retention
4. **Sports Partnerships**: League and team partnerships for credibility
5. **Social Features**: Community aspects and sharing capabilities
